Pocket Station - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026. Developer: Scarr (contact: scarrface010@gmail.com).

What the game collects

Leaderboard. If you submit a score, the game sends the player name you enter, a random player ID generated on your device, and your score/run stats to our leaderboard server. No real name, email, or account is required.

Save data. Progress is stored only on your device.

Advertising (Android app)

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. In the EU/UK and other required regions you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change this choice in the game. See how Google uses data from partner apps.

The web version shows no ads.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with Google AdMob as described above and with our leaderboard host to store scores.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Deletion

Uninstalling the app removes local save data. To remove your leaderboard entries, email us with your player name.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
